# Follow-speaker reframing

Approved scope: a dedicated portrait edit for a single clearly visible talking head. Sample at most 30 frames across an owned video of at most 60 seconds, request horizontal face positions from the configured vision provider and interpolate a bounded crop path. Ambiguous/missing faces, invalid provider data, missing configuration or cancellation fail the job rather than claiming a successful tracked crop. Multiple speakers, cuts and occlusion are not promised. Existing source overlays may crop; paired caption archives rebuild captions after reframing.

The free Studio control explains the limit and cost before submitting one normal versioned edit. Worker never trusts client coordinates, arbitrary filter text or storage keys. A dedicated immutable speakerReframe flag participates in retry content comparison. Archive and flattened video paths use the same media operation; audio is stream-copied and output decoded before delivery. Tests cover owner/billing/retry/claim, provider failure and cancellation, filter bounds, and a real moving synthetic subject rendered through actual FFmpeg. Mocked vision does not establish live tracking accuracy.
